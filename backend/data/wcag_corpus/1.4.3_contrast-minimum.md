# 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)

Canonical URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html

The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at
least 4.5:1, except for large text (3:1), incidental text, and logotypes.

## Why it matters

Low contrast text is unreadable for users with low vision, color blindness, or
on glare-heavy mobile screens. Gray-on-gray UI is a leading cause of
accessibility failures on e-commerce sites (placeholder text, secondary
buttons, disabled states, footer links).

## Common failures

- Body text in light gray on white, e.g. `#999` on `#fff` (~2.8:1).
- "Ghost" buttons with low-contrast outlines and labels.
- Placeholder text used as the only label for form inputs.
- Colored links that do not meet 4.5:1 against the background.

## Fix guidance

- Target 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for text ≥ 18pt or ≥ 14pt bold.
- Use a contrast checker (e.g. WebAIM) during design review.
- Don't rely on placeholder text as a label; it disappears on input and is
  usually below contrast.
- Ensure hover, focus, and disabled states also meet contrast.
