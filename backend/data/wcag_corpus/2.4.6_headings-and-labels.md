# 2.4.6 Headings and Labels (Level AA)

Canonical URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html

Headings and labels describe topic or purpose.

## Why it matters

Clear headings make a page scannable for everyone and provide the primary
structure that screen reader users navigate. Descriptive labels let every
user (including AT users) understand what a form field expects.

## Common failures

- Generic headings ("Section 1", "Details").
- Duplicate headings that could refer to multiple sections.
- Form labels that don't explain the required format or constraints.

## Fix guidance

- Headings should summarize the content of their section.
- Form labels should include the required format where useful
  ("Phone (10 digits)").
- Keep headings in logical order (h1, then h2, then h3).
