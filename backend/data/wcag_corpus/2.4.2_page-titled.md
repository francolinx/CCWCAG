# 2.4.2 Page Titled (Level A)

Canonical URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/page-titled.html

Web pages have titles that describe topic or purpose.

## Why it matters

The `<title>` is the first thing a screen reader announces and the text shown
in the browser tab / browser history / search results. A blank or generic
title ("Untitled", "Home") makes it impossible to tell pages apart.

## Common failures

- Missing `<title>` element.
- Empty `<title></title>`.
- Every page uses the same title.
- Title does not reflect the page (e.g. product pages all say "Shop").

## Fix guidance

- Give each page a unique, descriptive `<title>`.
- On product pages: product name first, then site name.
- Keep titles under ~60 characters where possible.
