# 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum) (Level AA, WCAG 2.2)

Canonical URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html

The size of the target for pointer inputs is at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels,
except for specific exceptions (inline, UA-default, essential, equivalent).

## Why it matters

Small tap / click targets fail users with tremor, arthritis, or using touch
on a small device. E-commerce sites often fail this on close-icons, variant
pills, and cart-step indicators.

## Common failures

- 16×16 icon-only buttons with no extra padding.
- Small "×" close buttons on modals / banners.
- Dense product-filter chips < 24×24.

## Fix guidance

- Ensure clickable targets are at least 24×24 CSS pixels, or provide padding
  that extends the hit area.
- Prefer 44×44 where reasonable (WCAG 2.1 AAA).
